Recommendation — the canvas edits my view; only Settings edits the workspace default. Making it the default becomes a deliberate act with a button, not a side effect of looking at something.

“Just for me” is the whole fix: it tells you the change is yours, and it makes Make default a decision you take on purpose. The dot is the same grammar the scenario chip already uses for “you are in a mode”.
Sequencing. The roadmap says this split should land with the toggles rather than after them, and that is right — but it is also worth doing on its own, before any new toggle exists. Two settings already write workspace-wide from a topbar. The split is a bug fix that happens to unblock a feature.
