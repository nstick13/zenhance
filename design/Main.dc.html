Is “on 2+ teams” a finding, a fact, or a filter?
Decided — it is a fact. Whether it becomes a finding is a per-discipline rule the organisation sets. There is no universally right number of teams, and no discipline whose answer we get to decide for them: one org reads a Security Engineer across six teams as the job working, another reads the same row as a bus-factor and burnout risk. Both are right about their own org.
