## What changed today

**The grow study wears the shipped map's clothes.** It now takes its *maths*
from `lib/orbital/{geometry,lod}` rather than copying the look, so the study and
`/org` cannot silently drift. People vanish at overview scale, a torus stands in
for a crowd, then real avatars with connection lines. Full write-up in
[LAB.md](LAB.md) § `grow`.

**Boundaries decide parentage.** Cross into a boundary and the node adopts that
parent; cross out of every boundary and it is severed; the innermost boundary
wins. The connection line under your hand while you drag *is* the question —
releasing acts, with no dialog. ⚠️ **This supersedes two lines in
UNIFIED-ORBITAL.md** ("Split off on release, never an automatic change", and
"on release, ask what should happen"). Greg replaced both on 2026-09-20. The
merge choices still ask, because two teams meeting is genuinely ambiguous.

**Northwind is gone.** The workspace and all its rows were deleted from the
**local** database, and the demo company, its seeding path and the code that
hid it are removed. Its *generator* survives at
`lib/orbital/__tests__/fixtures/deepOrg.ts` as a pure test fixture, because it
is the one thing proving the layout holds at ~2,500 people.

## Things that will bite you

- 🔴 **Nobody can look at a big org any more.** `AGENTS.md` used to say check
  every map change on three companies; it now says two, because the third no
  longer exists. Scale is arithmetic only. **A regression that only shows at
  2,000+ people will not be caught by eye.**
- 🔴 **Northwind was deleted from the local database only.** Whether the
  production Neon database still has that workspace was **not checked**. If it
  does, the removal of `isRetiredScaleDemoWorkspace` means it would reappear in
  the switcher for anyone who is a member of it. Verify before shipping
  `integration/orbital-map`.
- 🟠 **`/org` drag still persists an *arrangement*, not an org edit.**
  `saveOrbitalNodes` writes to `orbital_nodes`; `org_units.parent_id` is never
  touched. So the boundary/parent model in the study does **not** yet write real
  structure — `/people`, `/teams` and exports would still show the old shape.
  UNIFIED-ORBITAL.md names this as a pre-merge item and it is still open.
- 🟠 **`/grow-established` is a top-level route with no `requireWorkspace()`**,
  so on production it would be publicly reachable. Invented data only, so no
  house-rule breach, but it reads like a product route.
- 🟠 **Sum-of-areas sizing is flatter than `/org`'s 165 · 72 · 48.** That is
  Greg's literal instruction (area = 0.66 × summed child area) and it does cut
  against "look identical to /org". Unresolved, and his call.
